CoN Intel Overlay v1.2.6

Works on both the modern desktop client and the old HTML5 client. Open a match, then refresh after reloading the extension.

Open a Conflict of Nations match. A drawing panel appears over the map.

  1. Choose Pen, Arrow, Marker, Text, Range, Measure, or Eraser
  2. Hold Alt and drag (or click for Marker/Text/Eraser)
  3. Export a JSON sketch to share; teammates Import it in the same match

The overlay never captures the mouse. Strokes stay in map coordinates when you zoom or pan.
